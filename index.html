01 / The project

A home that could do more.

Gerald and Jennifer had an extensive wish list. After searching for an existing property, they realised the home they wanted would need to be designed around them. Working with Latitude 37’s in-house design and build team, they created a three-level residence that accommodates everyday living, entertaining and visits from family.

02 / The client brief

Space for everyday life. Room for everyone.

The brief balanced the couple’s day-to-day comfort with space to welcome children, grandchildren and friends. They wanted generous entertaining areas, a private main suite, practical connections between levels and dedicated spaces for their interests.

The result needed to feel cohesive, not simply accommodate a long list of rooms.

03 / The design response

Three levels, each with a purpose.

The main bedroom suite and principal living spaces sit on the ground floor. Upstairs, additional bedrooms and a rumpus room provide space for visiting family. The basement houses a theatre and golf simulator alongside garaging and storage.

A lift links all three levels. At ground level, the kitchen, dining and family areas connect to an alfresco space and pool, allowing the home to open up when people gather.

05 / Material and detail

Warmth within a restrained palette.

Natural stone, warm timber and dark joinery create contrast without competing for attention. The curved exterior introduces a softer architectural gesture, while skylights and high-level glazing bring daylight deep into the home.

The design also integrates smart-home wiring, automated lighting and blinds, and solar panels with battery storage.
